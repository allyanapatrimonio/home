✸︎about

A calm pair of hands for a busy feed.

I started out writing captions for a small ceramics studio and never stopped. Six years later I work with food brands, indie publishers and a few very patient founders, usually as the one person who touches strategy, shooting, editing and copy.

My rule is simple: if a post wouldn't make sense read aloud to a friend, it doesn't go out. I like clear briefs, slow mornings and a proper handover at the end of every project.
